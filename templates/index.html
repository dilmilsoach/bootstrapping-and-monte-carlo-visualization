Summary of above visualization

1. Bootstrapping

Resamples are taken from your actual data points. The process is non-parametric, so you do not need to provide any model in prior. No formulas, just raw evidence based on the observed data.

2. Monte Carlo

Generates new data from a mathematical model (Normal, Uniform, etc.). The model is selected in advance, based on prior knowldege of the data being analysed. It is parametric, i.e. follows a specific shape.

Have a look at Step 3! Notice that while Step 1 and 2 can look flat or skewed, Step 3 looks like a Bell Curve. Increase the sample size and check. So, the Central Limit Theorem kicking in again!!
